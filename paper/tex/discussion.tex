\section{Discussion}

Holding the public record as one structurally resolved space changes what can be
asked of it. A campaign that used to begin with account requests, bulk downloads
and format conversion begins with a query. A crystal is one row rather than six,
so counting is possible, and every value names the calculation behind it, so
comparison is possible. More consequentially, a repository can report only on its
own contents, whereas a resolved union reports on the field, and two of the things
it reports were not what a user of any single source would assume. Independent
sources computing the same formation energy for the same crystal at the same
level of theory agree within 50~meV\,atom$^{-1}$ barely two times in three, and
because the discrepancy is a
systematic offset rather than scatter it does not average away with sample size:
a corpus that merged the contributing columns would hand a model the offset to
learn as though it were physics. In the other direction, sources that agree to
the last decimal are usually carrying one calculation twice, so agreement between
repositories should be read as shared provenance until shown otherwise. Neither
observation requires new computation. Both require only that the record be held
in a form where the same crystal is the same row and every number remembers where
it came from.

For forward screening, the record moves the limit from where it is usually
placed. The route is not confined to materials somebody has already computed,
because a surrogate model ranks the uncalculated as readily as the calculated.
It is confined by the pool it is asked about and by the labels it was taught
from. A campaign screens one repository's holdings and trains on labels whose
providers disagree by the margins reported above, and both of those are
properties of the record rather than of the method. Running the completion over
the whole corpus, inside the domains where each property is defined, moves both
at once, and answers a question the field has not been able to pose: how much of
the known record has been passed over for want of a calculation rather than for
want of merit. Both answers are consequential. A large yield would mean forward
screening has been operating far inside its reach, with already-synthesisable
materials waiting to be recognised. A small one would mean the known set is close
to exhausted and progress has to come from designing structures rather than
finding them.

For inverse design, the record is the reference a claim of novelty needs, and
the same record serves the route in two ways that are easy to conflate. It
supplies what a generative model needs in order to work: deduplicated structures,
and labels that name the quantity they denote, so that generation can be
conditioned on a defined target rather than on a column heading. It also supplies
what such a model needs in order to be believed. The failures reported against
recent campaigns
\cite{cheetham2024artificial,juelsholt2026continued,szymanski2025baselines} were
not failures to generate but failures to recognise what the record already
contained, and a claim of novelty is only as strong as the reference set and the
identity criterion behind it. On this reading the two routes are not competitors
to be ranked. Forward screening is bounded by how much of the known set it is
asked about, inverse design by how much of the known set its novelty is measured
against, and both bounds are the same missing thing seen twice. Supplying that
record is what makes the two routes comparable, and eventually complementary,
rather than separate literatures.

What makes this maintainable is that admission and matching are the same
mechanism seen from two sides. A record that matches nothing is not an error to
be logged but the corpus's next member, and once admitted it becomes part of the
criterion by which the next record is judged. In this corpus 24.4\% of all matches
resolved onto crystals that assembly itself had added, approaching unity for
chemistries the starting pool did not cover, and the last source ingested, matched
against the largest substrate, found more than half of what it recognised among
them. The same relation reappears in the completion stage,
where twenty-one tasks can be gated only once earlier models have supplied the
property their domain is defined by. A space built this way has no final state,
which is what a merged snapshot cannot offer as its sources keep moving. Three
boundaries should be read with the results. Structural fingerprinting has a
domain of validity that enumerated framework libraries fall outside, and the
composite key that rescues them is a remedy for a known failure rather than a
general solution. Agreement is measurable only where two providers report the
same quantity under comparable methods, so the disagreement reported here is
measured on the part of the corpus most likely to be well behaved. And no value
was recomputed: every number is as its source reported it, and the union inherits
whatever errors those sources contain. What it adds is the ability to see when
two of them disagree. The full set of limitations, including the licence boundary
and the structural rather than geometric applicability gate, is
Supplementary Note~\ref{si-note:limits}.

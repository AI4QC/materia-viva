\title[Probing the limit of forward materials screening]{Probing the limit of
forward materials screening and the novelty claimed by inverse design with 14.6
million crystals resolved across 55 databases}

% ------------------------------------------------------------- ABSTRACT
\abstract{Materials discovery follows two routes. Forward screening searches
the crystals already known for one with the property wanted. Inverse design
generates a crystal to order and reports how many of its products are new. Both
depend on a question no public database can answer alone: is this crystal
already known, and is this property already known for it? The databases share no
key at the level of the crystal, so the same structure carries a different
identifier in each. Here we resolve that identity across 55 public databases and
keep, for every value, the source and the method behind it. The result holds
14{,}590{,}485 distinct crystals and 131.8 million values over 166 properties,
and it grows as it ingests, because a record matching nothing becomes the next
member of the reference. For forward screening, the record locates the limit.
It is not the number of known crystals but two things a campaign inherits from
whichever database it loads. Of 846{,}303 crystals carrying a formation energy from
more than one source at one level of theory, only 65.3\% agree within
50~meV\,atom$^{-1}$, and the disagreement is a systematic offset rather than noise. Of fifty properties
needing a calculation of their own, none has been computed for a tenth of the
pool. For inverse design, the record is the reference against which novelty has
to be judged, and recent campaigns have reported as new what such a reference
already held. The corpus supplies 513 bounded learning tasks to complete the
property layer over the whole record, and one identity criterion under which
novelty can be stated.}

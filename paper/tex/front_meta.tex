\title[Probing the limit of forward materials screening]{Probing the limit of
forward materials screening and the novelty claimed by inverse design with 14.6
million crystals resolved across 55 databases}

% ------------------------------------------------------------- ABSTRACT
\abstract{Materials discovery follows two routes. Forward screening searches known
crystals for one with the property wanted; inverse design generates a crystal to
order and reports how many of its products are new. Both are measured against
public databases that cannot be measured against one another:
the same crystal carries a different identifier in each, and the formula cannot
serve as a key because polymorphs share one. We resolve identity at the level of
the crystal structure across 55 public databases, keeping the source and the
method behind every value. The record holds 14{,}590{,}485 distinct crystals and 131.8
million property values, and it grows as it ingests: a structure matching nothing
becomes the reference the next source is matched against. What a
forward screen inherits from the database it loads is then worse than that
database can report. Independent sources computing one formation energy at one
level of theory agree within 50~meV\,atom$^{-1}$ only 65.3\% of the time, as
a systematic offset rather than noise; and of fifty properties needing a
calculation of their own, none has been computed for a tenth of the pool. The
same record is the reference a novelty claim needs. Auditing nine released
sets of generated crystals against it, the databases holding the few matches are
not the repository such claims are checked against, and the reported metric hides
the behaviour it is meant to rank: a generator 71\% unrecognised proposed a
composition nobody had computed once in 500, where random enumeration, 89\%
unrecognised, did so 82 times in 492. A novelty fraction measures the reference
and the criterion as much as the generator. This record supplies both, and 513
bounded learning tasks besides.}

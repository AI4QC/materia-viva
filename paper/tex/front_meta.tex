\title[A self-evolving materials database]{A self-evolving materials database
that unifies 54 public sources into one searchable space of 13.5 million
crystals}

% ------------------------------------------------------------- ABSTRACT
\abstract{Crystal structures are not scarce; a materials space that can be
searched, compared and traced back to its source is, and assembling one from the
public databases consumes effort that should go into screening. They share no key
at the level of the object they describe, so one crystal appears in each under a
different accession number, and a union merged once ages immediately because
every source keeps growing. Here we build that space. Identity is resolved at the
level of the crystal across 54 databases, every value keeps the source, record
and method that produced it, and admission and recognition are made one
operation, so a record matching nothing becomes the corpus's next member and at
once enlarges the criterion by which the following record is judged: the database
evolves as it ingests rather than being rebuilt. Assembly enlarged the
deduplicated pool by 64.7\%, and 15.9\% of all cross-source matches land on a
crystal it had itself added. The result holds 13.5 million distinct crystals and
121.6 million values over 166 properties. Redundancy is large and concentrated,
with 1{,}190{,}447 crystals described by two or more sources, and those sources
often disagree: of 870{,}683 with a formation energy from more than one, only
60.5\% agree within 50~meV\,atom$^{-1}$, as a systematic offset rather than
scatter. Coverage follows computational cost rather than scientific value, no
property needing its own calculation reaching a fiftieth of the crystals, so
screening the known record today ranks the materials somebody chose to compute
rather than the materials that exist. Predicting the missing entries, for which
the corpus supplies 497 bounded learning tasks, would measure the limit of
forward screening; the same standardised structures and labels are what
generative inverse design requires.}

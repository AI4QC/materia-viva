We thank the maintainers of the 55 source databases integrated here. This work
redistributes none of their data beyond what their licences permit, and depends
entirely on their decision to publish in the first place.

\section*{Declarations}

\bmhead{Competing interests} The authors declare no competing interests.

\bmhead{Data availability}
The unified database is published as a versioned dataset on the Hugging Face Hub
together with a development slice sized for interactive work. Five of the 55
sources are withheld from it: AFLOW's terms are academic and non-commercial,
ELEMENTA and GNoME are CC BY-NC 4.0, Inorganic Crystal Structure Database records
may be held only by a subscriber to the FIZ Karlsruhe licence, and NOMAD holds
author-deposited calculations whose licence is set per upload rather than for the
repository, so its values are withheld until the terms of each can be resolved.
All five are searchable in a database built locally from the published pipeline
and are filtered out before any public release. The database
contains no ICSD structure. The comma-separated data files behind every figure
are provided as source data.

\bmhead{Code availability}
The acquisition adapters, the fingerprinting and matching pipeline, the loader,
the completeness audit, the extraction scripts and the figure scripts are
available in the repository accompanying this work.
